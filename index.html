Commodity price benchmarks

Monthly market indicators alongside the wider commodity news desk — not a forecast of supplier costs.

About these figures. The World Bank Pink Sheet provides monthly international benchmarks, generally in US dollars; AHDB provides separate weekly UK cattle prices. It is not a forecast or a measure of Morrisons' or any other retailer's actual cost of goods. UK prices can differ because of contract terms, timing, exchange rates, specifications and supply chains. AHDB links provide more relevant UK industry data. Direction colours show input cost pressure, not investment performance. Missing values are shown as unavailable rather than estimated.
